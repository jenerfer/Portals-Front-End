radio selection

A radio is a round control that allows users to choose one option from a set

behaviour

Users can tap or click a radio to toggle between filled and unfilled. Selecting a radio will automatically deselect the previously chosen item since only one can be active at a time. Tapping or clicking the label next to the radio should activate the control.
